How the AI works

Physics-informed machine learning, trained on the plant's own melts

1

Measure practice from signals

Holding minutes, avoidable lid-open time, under-powered minutes and tap temperature, from meter power, the lid switch and the operator's log.

2

Recalibrate physics on this furnace

Each physics term (heat to spec, losses, overshoot) gets a coefficient learned from the plant's first 30 days, pulled toward the textbook value unless the data disagree.

3

Learn the charge

Gradient boosting on scrap mix, weight, grade and lining age gives the best-heat energy for a charge like this one. What's left over is charge quality: sand and rust.

4

Watch the bath live

A soft sensor tracks bath temperature from the energy balance and resets on every dip, so MeltWise can say “tap now” before the overshoot happens.
