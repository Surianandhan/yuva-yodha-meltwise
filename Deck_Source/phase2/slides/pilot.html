The pilot simulation. At 60 percent follow-through, kilowatt-hours per tonne fall from 657.6 to 635, a saving of 22.6, with a 95 percent confidence interval of 17 to 28. That is 4.3 lakh rupees and 38 tonnes of CO2 a year for one furnace. This is below our Phase 1 likely estimate, and we say so: the mechanisms are now modelled, not assumed.
