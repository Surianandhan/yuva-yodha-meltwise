About 5,000 foundry units in India, 90% of them MSMEs, made 15.86 Mt of castings in 2024–25. Most see energy only as a monthly bill, weeks after the melts that wasted it.

Five causes do the damage: overshooting the tap temperature, holding metal while moulds catch up, a lid left open, under-powered melting, and sand and rust in the charge.
