A foundry pays about 87,000 rupees once per furnace, plus 4,000 rupees a month. Against 3.7 to 9.5 lakh rupees of net savings a year, that pays back in one to five months. We sell to induction-furnace iron foundries through their cluster associations, starting with two clusters: one demo furnace and one local installer per cluster, and results shared with members. Cash-tight units can choose shared savings, and MeltWise's measured baseline makes SIDBI 4E loans for bigger upgrades easier to approve. At 1,000 furnaces, subscriptions alone would bring in about 4.8 crore rupees a year.
