Yuva Yodha Energy Tech Hackathon 2026 · Challenge 04: Smart Manufacturing

MeltWise

Every melt, measured.

An AI energy copilot that helps India's induction-furnace foundries use less power per tonne, with the same castings.

Surianandhan Sridhar (Team lead) · Sameera Hussainy Pattan

India's foundries waste electricity inside every furnace melt, and nobody sees it until the monthly bill arrives. MeltWise measures every melt, tells the operator what is being wasted while the metal is still in the furnace, and proves the savings to the owner and to buyers.
