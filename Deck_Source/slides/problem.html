India is the world's second-largest castings producer: about 5,000 foundries, 90% of them MSMEs, making 15.86 million tonnes in 2024-25. Studies of Indian foundries found melting energy 13 to 21% above industry norms, and energy can be 15 to 30% of production cost. Three changes make this urgent now: time-of-day tariffs since April 2024 reward melting in solar hours, global buyers ask for carbon data, and most SMEs sit outside PAT with no energy manager.
