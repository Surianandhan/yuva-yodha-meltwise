Roadmap

From prototype to 1,000 foundries

Oct–Nov 2026

Prototype

Melt simulator (physics + ML) tuned on published cluster data, plus operator app, dashboard and scheduler.

Target: predict melt energy within 5%

Jan–Jun 2027

Pilot

3–5 foundries in one cluster: 4 weeks of baseline melts, then 8 weeks with MeltWise, checked independently.

Target: 17+ kWh/t saved, verified

2027–28

Cluster rollout

50 foundries through an industry association, with local installers and a SIDBI 4E tie-up for upgrades.

Target: payback under 6 months

2028 onward

Scale

1,000 foundries, then steel induction melting, aluminium and forging furnaces, plus predictive maintenance.

Target: 80 GWh and 57,000 t CO₂ a year

Our ask: mentors in industrial energy, meters for the pilot, and introductions to foundry clusters.
