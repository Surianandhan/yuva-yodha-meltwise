4

MeltWise runs one loop on every melt. Measure: it reads the furnace's own panel meter every second, and the operator logs the charge on a phone. Detect: a physics-plus-machine-learning model predicts how much energy this particular charge should need, and when the melt goes over, it names the cause. Guide: it tells the operator, in their language, while the metal is still in the furnace. Plan and report: it schedules melts around the moulding line and cheaper solar hours, and reports savings and CO2 per batch.
