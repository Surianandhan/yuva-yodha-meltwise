This is the same one-tonne melt twice. Today it takes 75 minutes: part-power melting stretches it, and the metal holds for 15 minutes while moulds are not ready. BEE's cluster study found the same furnace swinging 50 to 80 kWh per tonne between melts from operating practice alone, and a monthly bill cannot show which melt wasted power. Our model uses conservative loss rates for each cause. With MeltWise, the likely case is a 62-minute melt at about 617 kWh per tonne instead of 650.
